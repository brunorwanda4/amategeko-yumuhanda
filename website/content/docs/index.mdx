**Amategeko y'Umuhanda** is an offline study app for the Rwandan provisional driving-licence theory exam.

- **Platforms:** Windows (desktop) and Android (mobile, ARM64, Android 8+)
- **Languages:** Kinyarwanda (default) and English
- **Offline:** no internet connection required — no ads, no analytics, no tracking

## Key features

- **Three quiz modes** — Easy (learning), Medium (timed exam simulation), Hard (strict exam conditions)
- **404 questions** with road-sign images, star bookmarks, and mistake tracking
- **Comprehensive stats** — attempt history, score chart, pass rate, and weak-question practice
