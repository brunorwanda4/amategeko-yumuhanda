## Modes

| | Easy (Byoroshye) | Medium (Hagati) | Hard (Bikomeye) | My Mistakes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Questions** | 5–100 random | 5–100 random | 20 weighted (images/complex) | 5–100 from your mistake history |
| **Timer** | Optional elapsed (no countdown) | Countdown (default 20 min) | Countdown (default 12 min) | Easy rules — no countdown |
| **Instant feedback** | ✓ green/red highlight + explanation | ✗ revealed on results | ✗ revealed on results | ✓ Easy rules |
| **Navigation** | Free back/forward/skip | Back/forward, flag, jump grid | Forward-only, no skip, no back | Free |
| **Answers locked** | On first choice | Modifiable until finish | Single confirmation, cannot change | On first choice |
| **Pass mark** | Shown after (configurable) | Shown after (configurable) | Shown after (configurable) | N/A |
| **Auto-submit** | No | On timer expiry | On timer expiry | No |

**Default pass mark:** 12 / 20 (configurable 10–20 in Settings).

## Timer warnings

Applies to Medium and Hard only:

| Threshold | Indicator | Banner |
|---|---|---|
| ≤ 25% time left | Warning colour + triangle icon | "Time is running out. &#123;time&#125; remaining." (auto-hides after 5 s) |
| ≤ 10% time left | Danger colour + alert icon + pulse | "Time is almost up! &#123;time&#125; remaining." (auto-hides after 5 s) |
| 0:00 | Danger colour + 00:00 | "Time is up. Exam submitted." (stays until dismissed) |

Timers are deadline-based: the remaining time stays accurate if the app is backgrounded or the phone screen locks.

## Resume an unfinished exam

If you close the app mid-quiz, a **Resume** banner appears on the Home screen. Tapping it continues the attempt from where you left off. Expired timed attempts are auto-submitted on next launch.
